# v0.0.15

BloodBuddy 0.0.15 can export a patient's readings as a PDF report.

## What is new

- **PDF export.** A PDF icon in the patient's history and on every row of the patient list opens the export. You can export all readings of the patient as saved, or the values grouped like the graph (Day, Month or Year, with the mean value per period). Either way the PDF covers all tests of the patient. Opened from the history, the export starts with the grouping the graph shows.
- **Laid out like a lab report.** The PDF has a provider block, a patient line, an export date and the period of the dates shown, and a table with the columns Parameter, reference range with unit, and one column per date, oldest first. Values outside their own reference limits are marked `[+]` or `[-]`. Many dates continue on following pages, and every page has a footer with the page number and a legend.
- **The app as provider.** The first page names BloodBuddy as the provider with the app version, the release tag, the build hash and the export time, and says that the document was generated from your saved readings and is not a laboratory report.
- **Share or save.** After the PDF is created you can share it through the Android share sheet or save it where you choose. The file is named `BloodBuddy_<name>_<date>.pdf`.
- **Your formats.** Dates and numbers in the PDF follow the date format and decimal mark you selected in the settings.

## Changed behavior

- The grouping you choose for the trend chart is now kept while you stay on the patient, so the export can start with it.
- A shared PDF is kept in the app's temporary storage until the next export or the next start of the app, because the receiving app may read it later.

## Privacy

- The PDF is created on your device and leaves the app only when you share it or save it. Nothing is uploaded, and no new permissions are requested.
- The PDF contains personal medical data. Share it only with people you trust.

## Known limitations

- There are no section headings like in a laboratory report, because the app does not store sections.
- Values are not converted: the same test in two units is two rows.

## Compatibility

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. The database format is unchanged, so existing patients and readings are kept. This release is signed with the same key as earlier releases.
