# v0.0.13

BloodBuddy 0.0.13 lets you delete saved data: single readings, whole days and whole patients.

## What is new

- **Delete a reading.** Open a result and use "Manage" to remove that one reading from the history.
- **Clear a unit or a limit.** In the same place you can clear a reading's unit (it then shows as "unit unknown") or its lower or upper reference limit, without deleting the reading.
- **Delete a whole day.** Remove all readings of the same day for the patient in one step.
- **Delete a patient.** Use the trash button on the patient list. The dialog shows how many readings will be deleted, and you must tick "I understand" before the delete button works.
- **Confirmation first.** Every removal asks for confirmation and says what will be removed. If the data has already changed, the app tells you and refreshes the history instead of deleting something else.

## Changed behavior

- Deletion is permanent. It removes the data from the history and from the app's database, and it cannot be undone.
- Removals only ever affect the selected patient. The statistics on the About screen drop by the removed readings and patients.
- Saved readings can now change in these cases: confirmed unit completion (as before), clearing a unit or a limit, and deletion. Names, dates and patients are never edited.

## Privacy

- Everything still works offline. Nothing is uploaded and no new permissions are requested.

## Known limitations

- There is no undo and no trash bin. Delete only what you are sure about.

## Compatibility

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. The database format is unchanged, so existing patients and readings are kept. This release is signed with the same key as earlier releases.
