# v0.0.10

BloodBuddy 0.0.10 makes importing laboratory reports more reliable, especially reports that contain results from several dates.

## What is new

- **One import path for every source.** PDF files, image files (JPEG and PNG) and camera photos now go through the same on-device pipeline. Image files can be chosen from the start screen and from a patient's screen.
- **Pages are straightened before they are read.** Rotated, skewed or photographed-at-an-angle pages are corrected automatically. When the automatic correction is not certain, you are asked to check or adjust the page (rotate, move the corners) before anything is read.
- **Better reading of tables.** Values are matched to their own column, so reports with one column per date, with dated sections, or with results spread over several pages are split into the right measurement days. Blank cells stay empty and are never filled in.
- **Review day by day.** Imported results are grouped by measurement date, oldest first, with a preview of the source cell next to each value. You can correct, add or exclude readings before saving. Dates that the report leaves ambiguous (for example `05/01/2024`) are not guessed: you enter them yourself.
- **Save each day separately.** Every measurement day is confirmed and saved on its own, for the one patient you confirmed at the start of the import.

## Changed behavior

- Reports used to be saved all at once. They are now saved **day by day**. If a day fails or you cancel it, nothing is written for that day, and days you already saved stay saved. If you stop after saving some days, the app tells you which days were saved and which were not.
- An import does not resume if the app is closed or killed in the middle, and the same report imported twice is not detected as a duplicate. Check the patient's history before importing a report again.

## Privacy

- Recognition runs on your device with the text-recognition model bundled in the app. Nothing is uploaded and no model is downloaded.
- Prepared page images are kept only in the app's private storage while an import is open and are deleted when it is finished or cancelled.
- No new permissions are requested.

## Known limitations

- The new reading logic was tested with invented sample reports only. Real laboratory layouts that differ from those samples may be read incompletely or wrongly, so compare every value with its source preview before you save it.
- The download is larger (roughly 150 MB more) because the image-correction library is included for all four supported CPU types.
- On Android 7.0, PDF import uses the system's older PDF renderer, which crashed once during our automated tests of many consecutive imports. If the app closes during a PDF import, open it again and retry, or import an image of the page instead.

## Compatibility

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Existing patients, readings, charts and the About screen counts are unchanged. This release is signed with the same key as earlier releases.
